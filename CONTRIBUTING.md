# Contributing

Thank you for helping keep this list accurate.

This repository is a curated list of **official** Indian resources on cyber fraud. It is not a general bookmark list.

## What belongs here

- A government, regulator, court or statutory-body URL that works (no 404) and is clearly official.
- A correction to a helpline number, a title, or a stale link, with a source.

## What does not belong here

- Unofficial blogs, Telegram channels, “scam alert” forwards, or commercial products.
- Tool code, detection methods, rules, thresholds or paid-pack material.
- Anything that tells a reader how to commit or conceal an offence.
- Advertising, guest posts, or link-exchange requests.

## How to suggest a change

Open a GitHub issue (or a pull request) with:

1. The exact URL.
2. A one-line description of the resource.
3. Why it is official (the ministry, agency or regulator).
4. The date you last opened the URL and the result (it must load).
